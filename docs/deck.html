Why a v2

v1 was too small, and its colours didn't map to anything we measure

  • Size: v1's 5 cm card leaves few pixels per chip; v2 is with chips.
  • Colours: v1/v1.5 used screen primaries. Several can't be printed, and none corresponds to a plant or soil colour class.
  • Red frame: competes with anthocyanin red in segmentation and pulls auto white balance. Removed.

Colour system

Every chip is a Munsell notation; the printed colour is computed, then measured

1 · NotationSoil-chart chips, Plant Tissue book hues, calibration hues.
2 · RenotationMunsell's published data → CIE xyY.
3 · Print + screenLab D50 for print and measuring; sRGB for the file.
4 · Gamut gateMust print (ΔE2000 ≤ 3) and fit sRGB, else chroma steps down.
5 · MeasureSpectrophotometer readings per batch replace the design values.

Rows A + neutral ramp

Calibration: anchors, a hue circle and a 12-step grey ramp

Gives the colour-correction fit something to grip on in every hue direction, plus white, black and N5 (≈ 18 % grey) for exposure and white balance.

Rows B–C

Plant tissue: leaf health, pigments, senescence and roots

Every hue is a page of the Munsell Plant Tissue Color Book. Value/chroma are design targets: we'd like consultants to confirm or swap them for actual book chips.

Soil: chips taken from the Munsell soil-chart pages, with their printed names

Hues 10R to 2.5Y. The low-chroma greys span grey-regolith darkness. No chip claims to match a specific simulant or Apollo sample; targeting simulants needs published Munsell values for them.

Corner markers

Standard ArUco markers: the card identifies itself

  • v1 already used OpenCV DICT_4X4_50 IDs .
  • v2 uses IDs , the best-separated block of four: at least 5 bits from any v1 marker, 8 from their own rotations.
  • Any ArUco reader (OpenCV, arucogen, our app) knows which card, which corner, and which way up.
  • Verified with OpenCV's own detector on both cards.

Printing, measuring + one v1 finding

From design values to measured references

  1. Print matte if possible.
  2. Measure ≥ 3 stickers per batch, every chip, with a spectrophotometer (D50 / 2°).
  3. Flag chips that vary by more than ΔE2000 2 between stickers.
  4. Measured Lab values replace design values in the app.
  5. Each print run gets its own batch number on the card.
